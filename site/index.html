Your phone follows your laptop.

Study with friends, and prove you were there.

Make an account, add friends by username, and see who focused most this week. Anyone can leave a timer running and walk off, so hush. counts verified minutes separately: during focus your webcam checks you're at your desk and not holding your phone.

The check runs entirely on your laptop with Google's open MediaPipe models. No video or pictures are saved or sent anywhere. Friends only see your focus minutes and streak, never what apps you used. The camera is off unless you turn it on.

What hush. can't do.

You own your computer and your phone, so a determined person can always get around a blocker: Task Manager, safe mode, uninstalling. hush. is built to stop the impulse to drift, not to be a prison.

The camera check stops "timer on, walk away". A photo held up to the webcam, or a phone hidden under the desk, can still fool it, and a friend who edits their copy of the app could fake their numbers. Keep the leaderboard to people you trust.
